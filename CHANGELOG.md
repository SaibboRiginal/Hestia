## 2026-10-30
- Hestia asks you questions in notifications too, the same way it does in the chat: Sì/No, one choice, several choices with «Invia», a free answer or «Altro…», with the same card on the site (Notifiche) and the same buttons on Telegram (where a free answer starts from «Rispondi…» or by replying to the message, never from what you write next); the first answer wins everywhere. A routine that needs to ask you something now asks this way and goes on with your answer (before, the question was lost). Answering by voice or by just writing closes the question, so the card no longer comes back after a reload, and in live voice Hestia reads the options aloud → `docs/work/2026-10-30-domande-nelle-notifiche/` [ext-chat]
- Hestia inside Claude: the repo is now a Claude Code plugin marketplace. `/plugin install hestia --marketplace SaibboRiginal/Hestia` adds a band above Claude's prompt with three modes: off (default, plain Claude, no context used), strumenti (Hestia's tools, Claude's voice), Hestia (Claude speaks as Hestia with her personality, your tone and instructions and what she remembers about you, read live from Hestia so a change there changes Claude too); Ricorda, Memoria, Stato, Sviluppo; Claude's own memory goes into Hestia only on command with a preview; a second plugin `hestia-dev` gives Claude Code the work protocol as skills and refuses edits to `.env` and `data/` → `docs/work/2026-10-30-hestia-in-claude/` [ext-chat]
- Safety rules for the assistant that do not depend on the AI: a question or a request for advice never changes anything, what a module saved is not copied into memory, nothing is deleted unless you ask, a new development starts only after your «sì», and at most six changes in one request; a blocked step no longer shows «Non riuscito» and an answer is never empty. Tried again with a small model in ten everyday cases → `docs/work/2026-10-30-ai-vera-gemini/` [ext-chat]
- Hestia starts building your own training dataset: every exchange you type is saved locally with exactly what the assistant saw and did (its instructions, the tools it used and what they answered, its reply), and your 👍/👎 now land on the right answer (from Telegram they used to arrive without the conversation, so they were useless). It also notices by itself when you ask again or say «no, intendevo…». A conversation you mark «Non usare per l'allenamento» in the history, or delete, never ends up in it; old turns go after a year (setting). Next: a stronger model corrects the answers at night and the export to train elsewhere → `docs/work/2026-10-30-dataset-allenamento/` [ext-chat]
- New «Umore»: Hestia remembers how you feel over time. When you say in chat how you are («oggi sono stanco», «giornata pesante», «sono carico») the assistant notes it by itself after answering, without asking (a small line «Umore segnato» shows it; «non segnarlo» takes it back); one tap from Home, the page or Telegram also works. The page shows the last weeks as colours, the trend and plain sentences on what goes with good and bad days; the evening recap mentions it and, if you want, one question a night «Com'è andata oggi?». Moods never end up in the long-term memory → `docs/work/2026-10-30-diario-umore/` [ext-chat]
- The assistant works with a small local model too: each request now carries only the tools it needs (about 50 instead of 250, a sixth of the text), so a 9B model on the PC no longer loses its rules; a person you tell about ("mia mamma si chiama Laura") goes in Persone and is no longer also copied into memory; tried with a small model in twelve everyday cases, and with models running out one after another → `docs/work/2026-10-30-ai-vera-gemini/` [ext-chat]
- The assistant tried in real conversations with a real AI across every module, and fixed what it did wrong: the same fact said twice no longer becomes two or three memories, a reminder or a birthday is no longer also saved as a memory, «dimentica…» really forgets (it used to say so and keep it), it no longer says «ho registrato» when it did nothing or promises «riproverò», a question like «mi consigli una pizza?» no longer records a dinner, a read («cosa mi metto», «la mia dieta») no longer shows «Fatto», «come sto andando» counts the run you just logged, «Case disponibili» and the house stats work again from every client, and a failing service is not called four times → `docs/work/2026-10-30-ai-vera-gemini/` [ext-chat]
- Hestia tried with a real AI (Gemini) on the cloud test bench, and what it showed is fixed: with Gemini no answer could use a tool (one tool description refused, the whole call failed); the default Gemini models no longer existed for new keys; «mettimi in agenda…» always failed; an action that failed was told as done; a routine was created three times; «niente da spostare» showed «Fatto»; settings and voice tools never reached the chat, so «rispondimi più diretto» became a memory instead of a setting to confirm (now a card «Caldo → Diretto»); an answer could be a raw «memory.search(…)»; no more «Buongiorno» on every reply.
